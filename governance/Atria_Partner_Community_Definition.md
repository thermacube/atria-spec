# **Atria Partner Community Definition**

## **Purpose**

This document defines the **type of institutions and organizations** Atria is designed to serve, and the **nature of partnership** Atria supports.

Atria is an open source project with a **deliberate scope and posture**.  
Partnership is defined by alignment and capability, not entitlement or volume.

## **Who Atria Is For**

Atria is designed for institutions and organizations that:

- Understand *free as in freedom*, not *free as in free beer  
  *

- Already operate and maintain open source systems responsibly

- Value auditability, determinism, and long-term ownership

- Prioritize institutional autonomy over vendor convenience

- Seek to avoid lock-in and predatory licensing

- Are willing to train staff to operate their own systems

- Can engage with a platform at the **execution and infrastructure level**, not just the UI layer

Atria assumes partners have the **technical capacity and culture** to support their own deployments.

## **What Partnership Means**

Partnership in the Atria ecosystem means:

- Deploying Atria in real-world environments

- Validating the system through actual use

- Identifying constraints based on evidence, not preference

- Participating in design discussions grounded in specifications and outcomes

Atria follows a **train-the-trainer** model.  
Partners are expected to build internal competence rather than rely on external operators.

## **What Partnership Does Not Mean**

Partnership does **not** mean:

- Roadmap control

- Governance by committee

- Bespoke feature development

- Operational or on-call support

- Help desk services

- Adaptation to procurement checklists

- Replacement of large, convenience-focused LMS platforms

When needs diverge materially, **forking is the appropriate and supported outcome**.

## **Supported Paths of Engagement**

Partners may engage with Atria by:

- Self-hosting and operating their own deployment

- Forking the codebase to meet local needs

- Participating in optional training or architectural guidance

- Contributing feedback based on real deployment experience

There is no requirement to upstream changes or maintain alignment.

## **Final Statement**

Atria exists for institutions that value:

- ownership over dependence,

- clarity over convenience,

- and integrity over speed.

Organizations aligned with these principles are welcome.  
Those seeking managed platforms, guaranteed support, or rapid customization should pursue other solutions.
