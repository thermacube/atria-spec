# **Atria Project Posture**

This section defines the **human, organizational, and ethical operating model** of the Atria project.  
It exists alongside the engineering specifications, which remain the **non-negotiable technical authority** for the system.

The documents below answer a different question:

> *How is Atria stewarded, adopted, and sustained without compromising integrity, autonomy, or people?*

## **Document Index**

1.  **Atria Stewardship Agreement (Private)  
    ** Defines the steward’s role, boundaries, authority, and definition of success.

2.  **Thermacube × Atria Deployment Requirements (Internal / Semi-Public)  
    ** Defines what Atria must enable for Thermacube’s hosted institutional contracts and what Thermacube will and will not provide.

3.  **Atria Project Governance & Open Source Position Paper (Public)  
    ** Defines governance, licensing posture, participation expectations, and authority.

4.  **Atria Partner Community Definition (Public, Short)  
    ** Defines the kind of institutions and organizations Atria is designed to attract.

Together, these documents function as **relationship contracts**, not legal contracts.

# **1. Atria Stewardship Agreement (Private)**

**Status:** Private / Internal  
**Audience:** Steward, Thermacube leadership  
**Purpose:** Protect sustainability, authority, and personal boundaries

**Summary of intent:**

- The steward is the final architectural authority.

- Stewardship is bounded (time, energy, scope).

- Forking is the correct response to misalignment.

- The project must remain life-giving, not identity-bearing.

**Core outcome:**

> Atria reflects the steward’s capability without requiring indispensability, overfunctioning, or emotional labor.

*(This document is not published, but is referenced internally whenever pressure or ambiguity arises.)*

# **2. Thermacube × Atria Deployment Requirements**

**Status:** Internal / Semi-Public  
**Audience:** Thermacube staff, aligned partners  
**Purpose:** Ensure Atria serves real revenue and operational needs

**Summary of intent:**

- Atria replaces WordPress + Sensei for hosted contracts.

- Feature parity + improved auditability is required.

- Migration must be conservative and trust-preserving.

- Hosted revenue is primary; adoption services are optional.

- Thermacube operates hosted environments; self-hosted partners own everything.

**Core outcome:**

> Atria raises the ceiling of Thermacube’s hosted e-learning business without increasing support burden or dependency.

# **3. Atria Project Governance & Open Source Position Paper**

**Status:** Public  
**Audience:** Institutions, partners, legal/procurement reviewers  
**Purpose:** Define governance, openness, and participation

**Summary of intent:**

- Atria is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later.

- Forking is welcomed; enclosure is discouraged.

- Specifications, not votes, govern behavior.

- There is no obligation to accept contributions.

- Atria is not SaaS, not vendor-hosted, not community-governed.

**Core outcome:**

> Institutions know exactly what they are adopting — and what they are not.

# **4. Atria Partner Community Definition**

**Status:** Public, Short  
**Audience:** Prospective partners  
**Purpose:** Filter for alignment, not volume

**Summary of intent:**

- Atria is for institutions that value ownership and auditability.

- Partners validate Atria through real deployment.

- Train-the-trainer is the engagement model.

- Partnership does not imply roadmap control or support.

- Forking is the correct outcome when needs diverge.

**Core outcome:**

> The right partners self-select in; the wrong ones self-select out.

# **Relationship to the Engineering Specifications**

The **Atria engineering specifications (0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3 and bridge specs)** remain the **technical contract** for:

- system behavior

- data integrity

- execution semantics

- integration boundaries

Nothing in the Project Posture documents overrides the specifications.

Instead:

- The specs define *what Atria does*.

- The posture documents define *how people relate to Atria*.

This separation is intentional.

# **Final Definition of Success (Integrated)**

### **Atria is successful when:**

- it operates correctly and deterministically,

- institutions can own and audit their learning systems,

- forks are possible without friction or hostility,

- and no one is trapped by the platform.

### **Thermacube is successful when:**

- hosted contracts operate with less friction than before,

- revenue is stable and ethical,

- training and expertise generate optional income,

- and the company is never forced into dependency-driven support.

### **The steward is successful when:**

- the work speaks for itself,

- involvement is chosen, not demanded,

- boundaries hold under pressure,

- and the project remains life-giving.
